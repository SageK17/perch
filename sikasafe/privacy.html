Privacy & your data

In plain language: what stays on your phone, what community mode sends, and what SikaSafe never does.

The short version. SikaSafe works fully on your phone. The messages you check are never uploaded. There is no account, no advertising, and no tracking. Community lookups and reports are off by default — they only happen if you connect a community server, and even then SikaSafe never sends your name or your own phone number.

What stays on your phone

SikaSafe saves a few things in your browser’s local storage on this device only. Nothing here is sent anywhere:

  • Your language choice.
  • The scam numbers you save in Reports, with any note you add.
  • A random app id (a random string, not linked to your identity). If you use community mode, it lets the server count distinct reporters without ever knowing who you are.
  • The community server address, only if you choose to connect one.

Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling removes all of it. You can delete individual saved reports any time in the Reports screen.

Checking a message

When you paste a message into Check, the analysis runs entirely on your phone. The text is never sent to any server, logged, or stored. Phone numbers found in a message are shown to you locally so you can choose to look them up or report them — nothing is sent until you tap one of those actions in community mode.

Community mode (off by default)

Community mode is only active if you connect a community server from Reports → Community server. It turns per-phone reports into a shared warning list. While it is on:

  • Looking up a number sends that number to the server to check whether the community has flagged it.
  • Reporting a number sends the number, the scam category, your optional note, and the random app id.
  • The server never receives your name or your own phone number. It combines your app id with your network address into a one-way hashed “reporter” fingerprint, and separately stores a hash of your network address — it does not keep your raw address or raw id.
  • Your free-text note is used only for moderation and is never shown to other users or returned by a lookup.

You can disconnect the server at any time to go back to fully offline and private.

About reported numbers

The numbers people report are other people’s phone numbers, so we treat them carefully. A number is only shown as flagged after several different people, on different networks, report it — one person cannot brand a number. One or two reports show only as “watch”.

If a legitimate number was reported by mistake, anyone can tap “Not a scam? Dispute this number” on the lookup result. A moderator reviews disputes and can clear a number; a cleared number then shows as reviewed and cleared and cannot be re-reported.

What SikaSafe never does

  • No adverts and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, or files.
  • No account, sign-up, or password.

Legal (Ghana)

A community server that stores reported phone numbers is handling personal data under Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
